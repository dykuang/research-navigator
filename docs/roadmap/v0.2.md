## Product objective

Build an **infinite research canvas** for exploring hypotheses, ideas, claims, evidence, experiments, scientific milestones, branches, negative outcomes, archive/revival candidates and relevant Agent session references.

Canvas is **a view and controlled editing surface for canonical research state**, not a second independent research database. The product supports five existing modules (topic, literature/baselines, experiments, pivots, synthesis) without losing their state and evidence safeguards.

## Core architectural invariants

1. Canonical project state remains rooted under `research/state/`; schema additions must be backward compatible or explicitly migrated. Keep stable IDs and append-only history.
2. Store presentation-specific node positions, collapsed branches, saved views and viewport separately under `research/canvas/`. Position changes are not scientific decisions.
3. Scientific statuses stay orthogonal: workflow, epistemic, review, outcome, lifecycle, impact. A completed task is not a validated claim.
4. Research branches form a **typed graph**, not a strict tree: several branches may share the same trusted baseline/source or milestone.
5. Every accepted semantic mutation is validated by the engine and recorded in the event log. Canvas edits that affect research meaning are proposals until approved as appropriate; `--actor` is not identity authentication.
6. Archived ideas remain excluded from default positive planning but accessible for risk checks, synthesis and authorized inspiration. Revival requires a formal review and creation of a distinct new candidate with original negative evidence preserved.
7. SessionRef is only a **lightweight external pointer** (provider, project/workspace reference, session ID, role, link and provenance), not full chat history; never invent Codex deep-link support.
8. Preserve existing offline read-only dashboard/SVG fallback when local writeback is absent. The future optional local editor bridge must bind only to loopback and have appropriate origin/CSRF safeguards.

## Release scope

- New typed ResearchBranch / scientific Milestone records, with explicit acceptance evidence, limitations and dependencies.
- Fine-grained Claim–Evidence provenance and session-to-research-entity links.
- Graph projection API, infinite pan/zoom canvas with typed cards, multiple views and stable-ID navigation.
- Persistent layout/viewport and safe read-only export.
- Controlled local writeback and human-approved research changes, including archive and revival.
- Student/mentor-centered filters and real-world continuity across sessions.

### Out of scope

- Automatic ingestion or interpretation of complete Codex/Agent chat histories; conversational decision replay is targeted to v0.3.
- Full online multi-user collaboration and production identity/authentication.
- AI-only approvals, automated paper truth verification or experimental outcome fabrication.
- Changes to GitHub source code branching as a replacement for research branch entities.

## End-to-end demonstration and graduation criteria

1. Migrate an older research project. All original IDs, evidence, archived records, decision events and CLI semantics remain intact.
2. Create three research branches A/B/C and a scientific Milestone, sharing at least one baseline/evidence item without duplicate canonical records.
3. Explore/pan/zoom/drag/expand/filter/save canvas and reload. Saved layout must restore; scientific state must not change on drag.
4. An inconclusive/negative result for A is recorded with scope. Human authorizes park. A is removed from positive planning but still retrievable for lessons, risk review and synthesis. Its dependent Milestone becomes needs-review; shared baseline in B remains usable.
5. Two sessions relate to A; one of them also relates to C. Inspector shows source/role/time. Validated link opens on user action; unknown provider deep link only copies ID.
6. User asks to *reference* A (read-only, no mutation), then explicitly asks for revival review and separately approves a new candidate. Old A stays archived, new candidate is uncertain/hypothesis with derived-from/revives links.
7. Semantic edit concurrency, approval gates, path/origin safety, Windows/Codex smoke checks, old CLI tests, offline snapshot and browser keyboard access pass with recorded evidence.
